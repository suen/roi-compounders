Why Incorporate? The Tax Leverage

The primary driver for establishing a German Investment GmbH ("Trading GmbH") is the massive difference in taxation on share deals. While private individuals pay approx. 26.4% (Abgeltungsteuer + Soli), a GmbH pays effectively ~1.54% on gains from selling shares in other corporations.

Key Takeaway

This 25% liquidity advantage allows for faster compounding of capital within the company wrapper. Note: This applies to gains on disposal (selling stocks). Dividends are taxed differently but can also be privileged.

The Price of Entry: Capital & Fees

To form a GmbH, you need significant share capital. While a UG (Mini-GmbH) requires only €1, it is generally not recommended for serious investment vehicles due to image and acceptance issues with brokers. Below is the breakdown for a standard GmbH setup.

  • Share Capital (Stammkapital)

    Total €25,000 required. At least €12,500 must be liquid in the bank account at founding.

  • Notary & Court Fees

    Approx. €800 - €1,000 depending on the complexity of the Articles of Association.

  • Business Registration

    Trade Office (Gewerbeamt) registration costs vary by city, approx €30 - €60.

The "Cost of Doing Business"

A GmbH is not free to run. It requires double-entry bookkeeping and annual financial statements. To make the 1.54% tax rate worth it, your tax savings must exceed these running costs.

Tax Advisor (Steuerberater)

€2k - €4k / year

Depends on the number of trades and complexity. High-frequency trading increases booking costs significantly.

IHK & Admin

~€300 / year

Mandatory Chamber of Commerce (IHK) membership fees and annual LEI renewal fees.

Break-Even Point

~€100k portfolio

Generally, a portfolio size of €100k+ or annual profits >€10k are needed to justify the administrative overhead.
